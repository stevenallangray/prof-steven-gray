                  SUMMARY OF SCHOLARLY CONTRIBUTIONS
       I am an environmental social scientist who studies collective intelligence,
knowledge integration, and computational approaches to environmental
decision-making. My scholarship examines how individuals and groups
understand complex social–ecological systems and how differences in knowledge
influence collective understanding, decisions, and outcomes. My research has
contributed to theoretical and methodological advances in wisdom-of-crowds
research, knowledge diversity, systems thinking, and participatory modeling. I
have published over 100 articles in journals that include Nature Sustainability,
Nature Ecology and Evolution, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences,
Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment, BioScience, and Ecological Applications.
I also developed and maintain Mental Modeler (www.mentalmodeler.com), a
freely accessible software platform that represents, integrates, and analyzes
mental models for decision-making that has been used in more than 200 peer
reviewed studies and contexts that cover a wide range of disciplines.
       Applications of this research are broad and addresse a wide range of use
cases including climate adaptation, flooding risks, biodiversity conservation,
fisheries governance, coastal resilience, and sustainable food systems among
others. This work has received more than 9,300 citations (h-index 47), and I have
secured more than $6 million in external research funding as principal or co-
principal investigator.
       I am currently a Professor at Michigan State University and hold a
Research Scientist appointment at Johns Hopkins University's Whiting School of
Engineering, funded through the National Institute of Standards and Measures
(NIST). Previous appointments include Research Scientist at NIST, Associate
Fellow at the IT University of Copenhagen and tenure track positions at the
University of Massachusetts and the University of Hawaii. I have contributed to
multiple national environmental assessments (i.e. National Climate Assessment
and National Nature Assessments) and NSF funded synthesis groups in addition
to several international scientific collaborations including pro-longed research
stays abroad at the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries
in Berlin. My academic record reflects sustained contributions to environmental
social science through theoretical development, computational methodological
